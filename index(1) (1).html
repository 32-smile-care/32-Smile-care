Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you contact the clinic.

How can I ask for an appointment?

Call 9155010032 or use the WhatsApp appointment link on this page. Please confirm the date and timing with the clinic.

Where is 32 Smile Care located?

The clinic is listed at Indra Nagar Rd, Sarweshwari Nagar, Pahartoli, Ranchi, Jharkhand 834005. Use the map below for directions.

Can you tell me the exact treatment cost online?

Costs can depend on the clinical findings and treatment plan. Contact the clinic for an enquiry; a final plan and estimate should be confirmed after assessment.

Do all toothaches need a root canal?

No. Tooth pain can have different causes. A dental examination, and sometimes imaging, is needed to identify the cause and discuss suitable treatment.

Can mouth symptoms be related to general health?

Some oral changes can have several possible causes. Persistent, unusual or concerning symptoms should be assessed by a qualified dental or medical professional.
